Platform Features

Everything researchers actually need

🔬

Replication Tracking

Vote-based replication success rates aggregated from real attempts, with visual dashboards showing community consensus.

✍️

Rich Text + Math

Full editing with bold, italic, LaTeX equations (KaTeX), image embeds, and blockquotes for rigorous scientific writing.

👤

Author Verification

Paper authors can verify via institutional email and post with a verified badge, offering direct clarification.

▲▼

Up/Down Voting

Community-driven quality signals. Upvote helpful reports, downvote misleading claims — net score displayed prominently.

🏷️

Structured Categories

Tag posts as Full Replication, Partial, Failed, Methodology Note, or Discussion for filtering and meta-analysis.

🤝

Threaded Replies

Respond directly to specific posts and build nuanced scientific conversations that go beyond simple comments.
